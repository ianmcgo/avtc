Delete your account?

This cannot be undone. Your sign-in, username, public profile, meetup seats, chat messages, game confirmations and badge progress are deleted.

Meetups, listings and games you posted stay for the club but show as Deleted member.

Join the club records?

Make your account public and your username will appear in the Club Members list on the Record tab. Signed-in members can see that list and the games you have recorded. Your email is never shown.

You can change this any time in Settings.
